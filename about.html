About this site

This site explains the 2027 Medicare and Social Security changes in plain language, with a calculator that shows what they mean for your check.

Who it's for

People on Medicare or Social Security, and the family members who help them plan. Text is set large, the calculator runs without sign-up, and every figure links to where it came from.

How we check the numbers

  • Official sources first. We use Social Security (ssa.gov), CMS, and Medicare.gov wherever we can.
  • Reputable reporting second. Where the official page isn't yet available, we note which outlets reported the figure.
  • Status shown. The Sources page lists every figure and whether it has been confirmed on an official site.
  • Projections replaced. Forecasts are replaced by official figures as soon as they are published.

Who's behind it

This site is run independently by its editor. Each figure is checked against an official source, and the Sources page shows where it came from.

Independence

This site is not affiliated with CMS, the Social Security Administration, or any insurer or plan. We don't sell Medicare plans. This site shows ads served by Google AdSense, and every ad is labeled as an advertisement.

Keeping it current

The numbers change once a year. Social Security announces the cost-of-living adjustment in October, and CMS announces the Medicare premiums and deductibles in the fall. We update the site when those figures are published, and we show the date of the last check at the bottom of every page.
